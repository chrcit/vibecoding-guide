# Journey lessons on a phone — neon (lessons 15–21 + `/journey/week/`)

Tested at `390×844` (`deviceScaleFactor: 2`, `isMobile: true`, `hasTouch: true`) in light and dark `colorScheme`, plus `1440×900` desktop reference, against `http://127.0.0.1:4826/`. Screenshots (178 PNGs) saved under `~/.local/state/agent-artifacts/vibecoding-guide/journey-mobile/neon/<chapter>/`. Dark mode (`rgb(22, 21, 18)` background, `rgb(240, 235, 225)` ink) matches light mode's layout cleanly with zero horizontal document overflow (`docScrollW = 390px`) and no unstyled light boxes.

Across all eight pages, `.j-view` (`position: sticky` under the `57px` site header) stacks `.j-rail` (`40px`) + `.j-screen` (`104px`, `73px` message log or `42px` in multi-thread mode) + `.j-stage` (`228px`) + `.j-cap` (dynamic height). Total sticky height runs `456–696px`, leaving only `148–388px` below `.j-view` for step text and controls. Removing `.j-screen` and `.j-cap` recovers `240–390px` immediately, leaving the animated picture (`228px`) plus step navigation with room for a readable scroll container below.

## Navigation on a phone (recommendations)

Removing `.j-screen` and `.j-cap` drops the top sticky visual from `~600px` down to just the picture (`~228px`) plus a compact navigation strip (`~44–48px`), freeing **`~510px`** of vertical space on a `390×844` phone — more than `3×` the `148–190px` currently left on `proof`, `jobs`, and `grow`.

1. **Replace scroll-spy with explicit Back / Next step navigation on mobile**:
   - Place a single full-width bar directly under `.j-stage` (between the picture and the scrollable step-text container): **`← Back`** (`min-width: 88px, height: 44px`), a centered step indicator **`2 / 5`** (or tappable step dots / compact step title), and **`Next →`** (`min-width: 88px, height: 44px`).
   - Putting Back/Next right under the illustration keeps the thumb near the natural middle-lower grip zone, lets the reader flip animations without losing their place, and cleanly separates **"change step"** from **"scroll the current step's text"**.
   - On the final step of a lesson (`5 / 5`), change **`Next →`** (or keep `<Next>` at the bottom of step 5's scroll container) to advance to the next lesson (`Next: Proof →`).
