# Journey mobile test · sol

Tested the running Portless site on branch `skill-and-mobile-lessons` at 390×844 CSS px and 2× pixel density, with light and dark appearance. The T3 browser used a desktop user agent and reported zero touch points. This covers layout, animation, scrolling and measured targets. Native touch and swipe behavior were outside the run. The notes below use the default Plain level. I sampled the chat, caption and readout three times per default step and inspected all step screenshots in both appearances. Longer-running examples have separate notes where applicable. Screenshots are in [the sol evidence directory](/home/arthouse/.local/state/agent-artifacts/vibecoding-guide/journey-mobile/sol).

## Navigation

- Put a control row with a stable height immediately below the phone illustration. Use labelled Back and Next buttons with large tap targets, plus a short step-position indicator. Keep those controls visible while the text panel scrolls independently. A navigation press should select the step, start its animation, and return the text panel to the start of its new heading. Keep Replay inside the step when the animation benefits from it.
- Keep vertical scrolling for the step text, controls, and open disclosures. Leave swiping out of the first version. A horizontal gesture would compete with the lesson rail, range sliders and scrollable prompt blocks, while the visible buttons already give a complete progression path.
- Keep exact overview jump links and make their targets select the corresponding step and show its heading. Opening the “Only the brief” target at `/journey/handoff/#brief` activated the correct animation, but the frame hid the heading, instructions and comparison buttons. The colour legend and Next card were the first visible content below the frame. Keep “Open lesson” prominent on each overview card. Preserve the three part links and the final handoff to the next part. The Part 2 overview is the overview covered in this run.
- The active lesson rail is 358 px wide on the phone, with 496–550 px of contents across the sampled pages. Its small numbered targets and hidden scrollbar make it a poor step control. Use it for jumping between lessons. Use the last step’s Next card for the clear transition to a new lesson or part.
