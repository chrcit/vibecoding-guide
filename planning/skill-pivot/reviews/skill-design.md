Use reply numbers as checkpoints and finish the handover before the learner leaves the guide thread. ## Added after the walkthroughs (41 learner runs, three harnesses)

- **A general rule loses to a concrete one in the model's path.** "The reply checkpoints don't apply" in Setup was ignored in 6 of 6 returning runs on Sonnet. Concrete negatives placed at the reply-2 step held on Codex and Gemini. A fixed sentence marked "use as written" and a literal three-line template did better than "say how to start it", but the template was still dropped once on Codex.
- **Example values in a template get copied as facts.** The layers figure's sample lines ("ran 1 small script", "5 tool calls") reappeared as claims about turns where no script ran. The template now uses bracketed placeholders.
- **Harness behaviour is a fact to verify, not a default.** Antigravity under T3 Code loads none of `AGENTS.md`, `GEMINI.md` or rules folders into a new thread. Every sentence that promised carry-over had to be scoped, and one in `window.md` was missed until a later run.
- **The harness's own channels bypass "stay in the project".** Claude Code's auto-memory and session scratchpad, and claude.ai publishing from a plain thread, all needed naming explicitly.
- **Two correct rules can combine into a gap.** "Write exactly what you showed" plus "include the level only once it's confirmed" meant the level was never saved when the learner confirmed it in their yes. The fix is one named exception.
