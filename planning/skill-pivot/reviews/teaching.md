# Teaching in the tool

The guide sessions were Codex simulations with invented data. No real learner has tried the packaged guide yet. The earlier human coaching session informed its design. It showed how a spoken request could turn into a working analysis and how asking for a clickable form could change the lesson itself. In the simulations, an inline result on reply 2 gave the learner something concrete to change. “Put the place I should go first at the top” reordered the result immediately. Making instruments due today count as overdue changed a script and produced a check at the cutoff. Those visible changes are the testable part of the “it's just a sentence” moment.
