      What we bake

      The Tanner loaf

      A slow white sourdough, baked dark. Out of the oven by 07:00 every day we open, gone most Saturdays by eleven.

      Rye and caraway tin

      Dense, sour, keeps for a week. Sliced on request.

      Morning buns

      Cardamom knots on weekdays, cinnamon on Sundays.

      The night hatch

      Friday and Saturday from 22:00 to 01:00: hot sausage rolls, cheese twists and whatever the night bake has spare, through the hatch by the window.
